# Shared display

The display requires a normal household member session. Parents choose events and today's chores to share on the separate settings page, and can share meals when creating or editing them. Sharing an event applies to its entire series; sharing a chore applies only to that occurrence. A parent can remove either selection, including after the record leaves the display's date range. Records are private by default. The display sends selected event and chore titles, dates, times and chore statuses, as well as selected meal ingredients, source and recipe link; it excludes private meal notes, event and chore descriptions, locations, assignee names, balances and rewards. Skipped and paused unstarted recurring chores are hidden.

When `WEATHER_LATITUDE` and `WEATHER_LONGITUDE` are configured, the display also shows a weather card from the server-side National Weather Service provider: the near-term hourly forecast temperature, today's high and low when available, a short description, and the provider update time. The temperature is a forecast, not a live station observation. A snapshot past the fresh window is labeled as an older forecast with its retrieval time, and every available card credits NOAA / National Weather Service. Missing measurements are omitted, and a real `0°F` remains visible. If weather is unconfigured, times out, returns an unusable response or is otherwise unavailable, the card says so and the rest of the display continues normally. Weather uses one process-wide service with a 10-minute fresh cache, a 60-minute stale limit, background refresh with retries, and a circuit breaker; the 60-second page refresh does not cause an NWS request every minute. Aging weather is returned immediately while a background refresh runs. See [weather.md](weather.md).

With JavaScript, the page refreshes its data every 60 seconds; automatic updates can be paused. A refresh link works without JavaScript. There is no offline cache; a failed background refresh displays a connection message, while a failed manual reload uses the browser's network error. Expired or revoked sessions must sign in again. The display uses an ordinary account, not a restricted display token: anyone who can operate that device can also navigate to pages allowed by that account. Keep the device physically secure; do not leave a parent session unattended. A purpose-built restricted, revocable display session is future work.

Migration `0005_display` creates opt-in tables without altering existing records. Stop the service and verify a backup before upgrading. Actual device, tailnet and unattended-use behavior remain unverified. Calendar daylight-saving limitations in [calendar.md](calendar.md) still apply.
